# LordsRally — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is LordsRally?**
A platform for Lords Mobile: automated bot packages, ready accounts and codes (RSS), in-game bulk messaging, and a public ban wave tracker.

**Which languages are supported?**
Seven: Turkish, English, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian and Vietnamese — the whole platform, including bot commands and notifications.

**Do I need separate accounts for the different products?**
No. One LordsRally account works for everything, with a **single USD balance** shared across products.

## Accounts & balance

**How do I top up?**
Create an account, open your balance section and use one of the payment rails listed there. The rails shown are the ones actually open at that moment.

**Where can I see my spending?**
Your account keeps a transaction history: top-ups, orders, refunds. It is your own record — you can reconcile every movement.

**Why is a verified phone number required?**
Ordering requires a verified number so delivery notices (and account security messages) actually reach you. Verification is done once, with a code sent over WhatsApp.

## Bot packages

**Do I have to give my game account?**
No. Packages run on the platform's own bots bound to your guild/kingdom.

**Can I add extra capabilities?**
Yes — add-ons (such as automatic replies to players) can be attached to a package.

## Bulk Message

**Is Bulk Message WhatsApp/Telegram spam?**
No. Delivery is **in-game mail inside Lords Mobile**, to game accounts. Nothing is sent to anyone's personal messenger.

**How is the cost calculated?**
In credits, drawn from your USD balance, per delivered message. The rate depends on the message category and is shown **before** you send; it is fixed at approval.

**Can it get me muted or banned?**
Any repeated messaging carries risk in any game. The editor warns you about wording that raises mute risk, and the platform publishes its **real measured ban data** publicly instead of claiming it is safe → [ban wave tracker](https://lordsrally.com/ban-waves).

## Ban Wave Tracker

**What is a ban wave?**
A wave of bot bans that Lords Mobile applies across servers, usually within a short window.

**Where do the numbers come from?**
From LordsRally's own account pool measurements — the same data that drives the public page. Values that cannot be measured are marked as estimates; nothing is invented.

**Can I use this data elsewhere?**
Yes — the machine-readable file is in this repository: [data/banwave.json](../data/banwave.json). It is updated automatically.
