# Bulk Message — In-Game Mail Advertising for Lords Mobile

**Bulk Message** sends a message to many Lords Mobile players at once through **in-game mail**, and reports back who received it and who replied.

> This is not WhatsApp, Telegram or Discord spam. Delivery happens **inside Lords Mobile**, to game accounts, through game mail.

## Why in-game mail

- **It reaches the player where they play** — inside the game, not on an external channel.
- **You can target precisely** — by guild, kingdom, title or a hand-picked list of players.
- **It is measurable** — deliveries, failures and replies are counted per campaign.

## Targeting

| Source | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Guild** | Players of a specific guild |
| **Kingdom** | Players in a kingdom number |
| **Title** | Players holding a title (rank-based targeting) |
| **My lists** | Your saved recipient lists |
| **Paste** | Your own list of player names |

Sources can be stacked; the same player is never added twice.

## Campaign flow

1. **Write the message** — a title and a body, within the game's own limits. The editor shows the limits up front.
2. **Check the content** — the platform reviews the text and warns about wording that increases mute/ban risk.
3. **Pick recipients** — from the sources above; you see the final count and the estimated cost before sending.
4. **Send for approval** — every campaign is reviewed before it goes out.
5. **Track results** — delivery count, failures with reasons, replies, CSV export, and auto-replies to players who answer.

## Cost

Campaigns are paid from your LordsRally **USD balance** in credits. Credits are consumed per delivered message; the exact rate depends on the message category and is always shown **before you send** — the final rate is fixed at approval time.

## Frequently asked

**Will my account get banned?**
Sending too many identical messages is a risk in any game. The platform shows a **mute-risk warning** while you write and never sends more than you confirmed. Real, measured ban data is public: [ban wave tracker](https://lordsrally.com/ban-waves).

**Do I need to give you my game account?**
No. Delivery runs through the platform's own infrastructure.

**Can I see who replied?**
Yes — replies are collected per campaign, and you can attach an automatic reply.
