# Lords Mobile Accounts & Redeem Codes — RSS

**RSS** is the LordsRally product for buying ready Lords Mobile accounts and redeem codes, ordered from a balance-based shop.

## What you get

- **Accounts** — prepared Lords Mobile accounts, delivered as credentials.
- **Redeem codes** — codes you redeem in-game for the listed package.
- **Balance-based ordering** — you top up a USD balance once, then order from it. One balance works across every LordsRally product.

## How ordering works

1. **Create an account** on [lordsrally.com](https://lordsrally.com) — registration is free.
2. **Top up your balance** — the available payment rails are listed live in your account page.
3. **Choose a product** and order — the balance is deducted and delivery starts.
4. **Track the order** — status and delivery history stay in your account.

## Delivery and refunds

- Orders are processed against live stock; if an item is unavailable it is not silently substituted.
- If delivery cannot be completed, the amount returns to your balance.
- Every balance movement is listed in your own transaction history — you can always reconcile it.

## Frequently asked

**Is the balance shared with other products?**
Yes. LordsRally uses a single USD balance: bot packages, RSS accounts and Bulk Message all draw from the same wallet.

**What if I ordered a wrong item?**
Order status is visible in your account; contact support from there and the order is reviewed.
