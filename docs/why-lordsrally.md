# Why LordsRally — Measured, Transparent, Multi-language

Most Lords Mobile automation is sold with claims and no evidence. LordsRally publishes the parts that can be checked.

## 1. We publish our own risk data

Lords Mobile bans accounts in waves. Instead of claiming that automation is safe, LordsRally **measures ban waves** and publishes the results — timing, the share of a server's accounts affected, and how long recovery took.

👉 **[Ban wave tracker](https://lordsrally.com/ban-waves)** · source data in this repository: [`data/banwave.json`](../data/banwave.json)

Where a number could not be measured, it is **labelled as an estimate** instead of being presented as fact.

## 2. No invented numbers

You will not find:

- fake "customers served" counters,
- fabricated delivery guarantees,
- unverifiable claims about competitors.

Prices, availability and command counts are read **live** from the platform when you visit the site.

## 3. One account, one balance

Registration is free and one account covers every product. You top up a **single USD balance**, and every movement is listed in your own transaction history — top-ups, orders, refunds. You can reconcile it yourself.

## 4. Built for non-English players

The whole platform — including bot commands and delivery notifications — runs in **7 languages**: Turkish, English, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian, Vietnamese.

## 5. Automation that reports back

Bot packages do not run blind: events, delivery counts, failures and replies are recorded and visible in your account.
