## How ordering works

1. Create an account on [lordsrally.com](https://lordsrally.com) — registration is free.
2. Top up your balance — the available payment rails are listed live in your account page.
3. Choose a product and order; the balance is deducted and delivery starts.
4. Track the order — status and delivery history stay in your account.

If an item is unavailable it is **never silently substituted**. If delivery cannot be completed, the amount returns to your balance, and every movement is visible in your own transaction history.
