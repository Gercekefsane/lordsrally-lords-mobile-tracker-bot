# Lords Mobile Kingdoms — Tracking, Transfer & Status

LordsRally tracks Lords Mobile kingdoms and makes kingdom state visible: who moved in, population changes, migration windows and activity.

## What you can see

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Kingdom list** | Kingdoms with their recent activity and movement |
| **Kingdom page** | Players, guilds and activity for one kingdom number |
| **Migration view** | Kingdom transfer activity and the current migration state |
| **Inactive scan** | Players who stopped playing — useful for outreach |

## Kingdom transfer (migration)

Kingdom transfer lets a player or guild move to another kingdom, subject to the game's own migration rules. LordsRally bot packages include migration automation:

1. **Prepare** — the bot checks the target kingdom's migration state.
2. **Execute** — the transfer is performed when the window is open.
3. **Report** — the result is recorded in your account.

## Bulk outreach to a kingdom

If you want to reach players in a kingdom rather than move into it, the [Bulk Message](bulk-message.md) product targets by kingdom number directly.

## Frequently asked

**Which kingdoms are covered?**
Coverage follows live game state — the list on the site shows what is currently tracked. Kingdom-level (not player-level) information is public: [lordsrally.com/kingdoms](https://lordsrally.com/kingdoms).

**Do you publish player names?**
No. Player-level data belongs to the customer who is tracking it; nothing about individual players is published here.
