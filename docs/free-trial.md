## Frequently asked

**Do I need to give you my game account?**
No. Packages run on the platform’s own bots bound to your guild/kingdom; you do not hand over your game account.

**Do I need a phone number?**
Ordering requires a verified number (so delivery notices reach you). Simply watching the demo does not.

**Where is the trial button exactly?**
On the home page, the **See Live Demo / Demo** call to action leads into the experience.
