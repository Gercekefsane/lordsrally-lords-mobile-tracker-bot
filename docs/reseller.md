# Become a Reseller — Sell LordsRally and Keep the Margin

LordsRally runs a **reseller programme**: you sell bot packages to your own customers at a price you set, and you keep the difference. This page shows the **customer price** and the **commission** a reseller earns — the same figures every reseller sees on the public [salesman page](https://lordsrally.com/salesman).

## Why resell LordsRally

- **A ready product** — tracking, war support, wonder control, migration, RSS accounts and in-game Bulk Message, all on one platform.
- **You set the price** — the customer price is yours; your earnings grow with it.
- **One back office** — orders, balances and movements are tracked in the reseller panel.
- **Transparent risk data** — the public [ban wave tracker](https://lordsrally.com/ban-waves) helps you sell honestly instead of overclaiming.

## How to start

1. Open [lordsrally.com/salesman](https://lordsrally.com/salesman) and read the current tiers.
2. Register / apply there.
3. Share your packages with customers; orders and commissions are recorded in your panel.

> Commission and tier rules can change; the [salesman page](https://lordsrally.com/salesman) is always authoritative.

---

## Frequently asked

**Do I need stock?**
No. Delivery runs through the platform's own infrastructure; you bring customers.

**What is the difference between the customer price and the commission?**
The customer price is what your buyer pays. The commission is the part of it you keep. This repository never lists the platform's own costs — only the customer-facing numbers shown to you.
